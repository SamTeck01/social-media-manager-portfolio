# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
International brand managers and marketing leads (US/UK first) hiring a remote social media manager / UGC creator / voice-over artist. They usually land from a pitch DM, email or Instagram bio, most often on a phone, and decide in under a minute whether to book a call. Secondary: Nigerian brands.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Miracle Wealth Akhimien that turns visits from brands into inquiries. Success: a visitor thinks "she is seriously good" and a brand thinks "I can confidently hire her", then contacts her.

## Positioning
One person who handles strategy, content (scripting, editing, design, event video) and voice end to end, with real results for brands in Nigeria and the United States.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single scrolling page; Next.js static export in `web/`; deploy on Vercel.
- Mobile-first, fast, no jank. Video loads only on demand.
- Video grid and voice player exist but stay hidden until real media is supplied.
- Contact form composes an email (no backend). Public email, booking link, socials and domain are still undecided.
- Must offer both light and dark themes (user decision, 11/10/2026). Client asked for black, white and gold.

## Brand Commitments
- Client wants black/white with a gold accent, "not flashy, not too much".
- Name on site: "Miracle Akhimien" (to be confirmed by client).

## Evidence on Hand
- ADOSE Fashion (Abuja): 0 → 9,837 Instagram followers; Summer Shoes campaign 1,000+ pairs sold. Signed testimonial from Adesuwa Jane Oseghale.
- The Fly Foot Doctor (Houston, USA): scripts, editing, design, social; 800+ heel-care course purchases (client-reported). Testimonial draft not yet approved; channel totals are NOT her results.
- Taramoda Luxury: daily content creator; ~2K → 9K follower growth is self-reported and must not be published until approved.
- ParentalPal, The Covenant Nation Maryland: names only.
- One portrait: `web/public/media/portrait.webp` (1024×1536, cream background).
- Absent: videos, voice samples, extra photos. Never fabricate metrics, logos or testimonials.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over claims: every number shown is one she can defend.
2. One operator, three skills: strategy, story, voice from one person.
3. A phone visitor reaches the proof and the contact within two scrolls.
4. Restraint reads as premium: gold is an ink, not a glow.
