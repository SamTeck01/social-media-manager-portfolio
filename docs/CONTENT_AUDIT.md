## 3. Honesty Rules for the Numbers

The PDFs already hedge carefully, and the site should do the same.

- **Fly Foot Doctor channel totals (21.1K YouTube subscribers, 875 videos, 75.6K TikTok likes): do not present these as her results.** The YouTube channel is at least 6 years old (the first video is "6 yr ago"). Use them only as context ("a channel of 21K+ subscribers") and only if her dates match. Her result line is the course: *800+ course purchases*.
- **ADOSE:** safe to use 0 → 9,837 and 1,000+ pairs sold, because the client signed a quote that states both.
- **Taramoda:** say "grew from ~2K to 9K+" only after the client approves the testimonial. Until then, describe the work (daily content) and leave the number out.
- **Do not use the ADOSE infographic** (the purple "Summer Shoes Campaign" image) on the site. It looks AI-generated, including the person in it, and that undercuts real proof. Use her real posts, reels and screenshots instead.

## 4. Proposed Site Architecture (lean)

Her proof covers three solid projects, and nothing for voice yet. That is the "thin proof" case from the brief, so we go lean and expand later.

**INTRODUCE → SHOW → PROVE → HEAR → KNOW HER → INQUIRE**

| # | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Hero** | "Miracle Akhimien" (confirm what name she wants shown). Statement: *Strategy, story and voice for brands that want to be remembered.* Proof line: *0 → 9.8K followers · 1,000+ pairs sold · Clients in Nigeria & the US.* Portrait. |
| 02 | **Selected Work** | Three case studies with varied layouts: **ADOSE** (large, leads with numbers and the quote), **Fly Foot Doctor** (vertical video frames from her edits, "Houston, USA" tag), **Taramoda** (grid of her fashion content). Each one uses CLIENT / ROLE / WORK / RESULT. |
| 03 | **Capabilities** | Strategy / Story / Voice as three plain text columns. No icons. |
| 04 | **Voice** | Real audio player. **Include only if she sends samples.** Otherwise fold voice into Capabilities with a "samples on request" link. |
| 05 | **Clients** | Typeset list of all five names plus "and more across fashion, healthcare, faith and education." Text only, no fake logo wall. |
| 06 | **Kind words** | ADOSE quote now. Add the others once approved. |
| 07 | **About** | Portrait and short story, written from her bio. |
| 08 | **Contact** | "Have something in mind?", email, socials, booking link, short form. |

ADOSE is the lead case study because it has the strongest proof: signed numbers and a real sell-out. Fly Foot Doctor comes second as the international credibility beat.
