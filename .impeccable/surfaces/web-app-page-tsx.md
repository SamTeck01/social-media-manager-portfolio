# Surface: Portfolio home (web/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Experience. Audience: international brand managers on phones, deciding in under a minute. Action: inquire (contact form / email). Proof: ADOSE (signed), Fly Foot Doctor (Houston), Taramoda; client list. Constraints: black/white/gold pinned by client; light AND dark themes required; video/voice hidden until supplied; no fabricated numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a solo exhibition, "Strategy · Story · Voice", where every client is a hung work with a museum wall label. It refuses the category default of hero + stat cards + service grid + testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Gallery architecture. Light = white cube (paper white #F6F4EF wall, ink #121110). Dark = the same room after hours (#0E0D0C wall, #EEE9E0 text). Gold (#B8955A light / #C9A96A dark) only as gilt: label numbers, a 1px plaque rule, the lights switch. Type: a high-contrast display serif for titles, a neutral grotesk set small for wall labels with tabular figures. No cards, no shadows, no rounded corners; generous wall space; labels sit beside works, bottom-left aligned like real placards.

STORY: Visitor walks in, reads the title wall, sees who she is and where she has shown (Nigeria, USA), walks past three works with their labels and results, reads the curator's note (about), signs the guestbook (inquiry).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: exhibition title set huge across the left two-thirds ("Miracle Akhimien" + "Strategy, Story & Voice"), dates/venue line in label type; portrait hung right at ~38% width with a wall label under it. Top bar: name, room links, lights switch. Primary action "Commission work" sits under the title. Mobile: title, portrait full-width with label, action visible by the second scroll.

FORM: Exhibition / white-cube gallery, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key bb3090c3. Signature interaction: the lights switch (theme toggle) dims the gallery with a slow cross-fade; labels reveal as you walk past.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
